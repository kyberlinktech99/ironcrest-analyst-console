IRONCREST / PAIR BRIEF / VERSION 2

Keep Talking and Nobody Gets Breached

Case IC-27 · 35 minutes · Two people, one shared outcome

A contractor’s urgent request has triggered an investigation at Ironcrest. Your pair must verify the visitor request, trace a physical exposure, build and revise a risk register, and fund a defense plan before the incident window closes.

Your roles

Chromebook Operator: open the assigned game link in its own tab, inspect evidence and enter your pair’s decisions. Describe what you see; do not take the printed manual.

Printed-manual Analyst: interpret the Operator’s observations using the reference tables. Ask for exact identifiers, explain the applicable rule, and verify the read-back. Do not view the screen in standard play.

Exchange roles after evidence update Alpha. You are a team: neither person should decide silently.

Mission sequence

  1. Verify the request: identify the entry attempt and use the visitor protocol.
  2. Trace the exposure: inspect the floor plan and construct an evidence-supported route. Reassess when Alpha arrives.
  3. Build the risk register: compare two assets. Preserve the baseline and reassess when Bravo arrives.
  4. Fund the defense: choose complementary controls within budget, then explain residual risk.

How play works

Validation gives feedback without deleting your work. Hints are available without a time penalty; their use appears in your report. Alerts do not interrupt typing. Earlier findings stay in the evidence ledger.

The clock continues if you reload or close the tab. Progress is saved on this browser profile only. Use one tab and one Chromebook per pair. Ask your teacher before pausing. Do not enter real names, passwords or personal information.

At completion or timeout, copy the report into the Schoology assignment or use Print / Save PDF. A report is not submitted automatically.

Class timing

5 minutes briefing → 35 minutes play → 5 minutes debrief and submission.

Accessibility: all tasks support keyboard input and visible text. Ask for larger print, an accessible digital manual, shared viewing, a reader or adjusted timing as needed. These accommodations preserve evidence-based reasoning.

Debrief: What changed your priority? Which assumption did your partner catch? Which control could fail, and what would you monitor?
