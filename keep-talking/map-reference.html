How to read the facility map

Both partners may use this sheet. It explains location labels. The Operator still supplies live observations, and the Analyst still interprets door states with Manual B.

Three different kinds of identifiers

ExampleWhat it means
D by itselfRoom D, which is Storage.
D4 or Door 4 (D4)Door number 4. The D in a door ID means “door.” It does not mean room D.
D–ERoom D to room E. The dash means the rooms connect. Door 5 (D5) sits on this connection.
M7A shelf identifier in Media vault E. It is neither a room nor a door.

Read an observation aloud

“D5 connects D–E: circle marker” means:
“Door 5 connects Storage, room D, to Media vault, room E. The observed marker is a circle.”

The Analyst uses Manual B to explain the marker. A connection tells you where you could go; current door evidence tells you whether you can pass through it.

For your route: enter room letters with the Add buttons. Inspect the rooms, report the exact door observations, and check every connection with your partner. Do not assume two rooms connect just because their boxes touch on the schematic.
