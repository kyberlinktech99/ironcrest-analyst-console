1.3.B + 1.3.C
ATTACK

EVIL TWIN

An adversary-operated wireless access point uses an SSID similar or identical to a legitimate network. The victim may connect because the network appears familiar or stronger.

WHAT SUPPORTS IT

Observable evidence

Near-match SSID + unapproved BSSID + client association + interaction with the rogue network provides a much stronger classification than the SSID name alone.

DO NOT OVERCLAIM

Evidence boundary

A fake portal proves a credential request occurred. A submitted training form establishes exposure in this simulation. Neither fact alone proves those credentials caused later account access.

PROTECTION

Verify before you trust

Verify the exact SSID and consider data sensitivity before using public Wi-Fi. A VPN encrypts traffic to the VPN operator, but it does not make a fake credential portal trustworthy.
