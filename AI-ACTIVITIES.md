# Unit 1 AI activities

- U1-004 Borrowed Voice: Topic 1.4, AI-Based Cybersecurity Attacks. Practice verification, information handling, and limits on AI attribution. Twelve defender records, six optional sources, three evidence credits.
- U1-005 Signal or Noise: Topic 1.5, Leveraging AI in Cyber Defense. Practice contextual triage, model limitations, review of configuration/code/rule suggestions, and proportionate response. Fourteen defender records, six optional sources, three evidence credits.
- Each has seven guided stages, the shared AP Lens and terminology reference, a separate defender case, and eight core FRQ subparts plus an optional Analyze Risk extension. Recording unlocks enrichment; it does not automatically score or submit to a teacher.
- The landing page now includes activities for all five Unit 1 topics. These are focused reinforcement activities, not an exhaustive treatment of every essential-knowledge statement.

## Alignment sources (reviewed September 27, 2026)

- [College Board AP Cybersecurity CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-cybersecurity-course-and-exam-description.pdf): printed pages 39–42, objectives 1.4.A–B and 1.5.A–B. Guided content and cases are original classroom practice. The final source-based tasks also practice Detect Attacks; Topic 1.4's suggested skills are Analyze Risk and Mitigate Risk.
- [Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity](https://www.netacad.com/courses/introduction-to-cybersecurity?courseLang=en-US): foundational sections 2.2/3.4 for AI deception and information handling, 4.1/4.2 for defense. These are editorial applications of foundational concepts, not claims of dedicated AI lessons.
- [CYBER.ORG Cybersecurity 1](https://cyber.org/cybersecurity-1): foundational Units 2/9 and 8/9 respectively. Actual lessons may require teacher access to CYBERPortal.

## Answer ordering

Shared Fisher–Yates shuffling copies each choice list and preserves its stable identifier. Each guided attempt generates its own orders; back navigation and outcome reveals reuse them. Signal Lost checkpoints and the Wi-Fi decision use the same policy. Short drills shuffle when opened. The correct answer may appear in any position, including first; no fixed positional pattern is imposed. Source chronology, team menus, and written FRQ subparts retain their meaningful order.
