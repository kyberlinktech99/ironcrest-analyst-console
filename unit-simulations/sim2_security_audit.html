Physical Security Audit
You're a physical security consultant. Three different organizations have hired you to assess their facilities. For each site, read the description, identify the real vulnerabilities, and recommend the right controls. Not every item in the list is a vulnerability — red herrings included.
// YOUR ROLE
You'll read site descriptions, identify which items are genuine vulnerabilities, and for each vulnerability select the BEST remediation control. Some items may look like vulnerabilities but aren't. Choose carefully.
// KEY CONCEPTS
PHYSICAL SECURITY AUDIT
A systematic assessment of physical controls protecting a facility. Covers access control, surveillance, environmental controls, and procedures. Identifies gaps before attackers do.
COMPENSATING CONTROLS
Alternative controls used when the primary control isn't feasible. Example: if a mantrap isn't possible, adding a security guard + camera + visitor escort is a compensating control set.
RESIDUAL RISK
The risk that remains after controls are applied. No control eliminates 100% of risk — the goal is to reduce risk to an acceptable level for the organization's risk tolerance.
RED HERRING FINDINGS
Not everything that looks like a vulnerability actually is one. A professional auditor must distinguish real risks from non-issues — recommending unnecessary controls wastes budget and creates friction without reducing risk.
