// KEY CONCEPTS
CONFIDENTIALITY
Ensuring information is accessible only to those authorized to see it. Violated by unauthorized access, data leaks, credential theft.
INTEGRITY
Ensuring data is accurate, complete, and hasn't been tampered with. Violated by unauthorized modification, corruption, or falsification.
AVAILABILITY
Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed. Violated by DDoS attacks, ransomware lockouts, hardware failures.
CIA TRIAD
The foundational framework for information security. Most real breaches violate multiple pillars simultaneously.
IMPACT ANALYSIS
Determining which pillar is MOST affected helps prioritize response. Not all violations are equal.
DEFENSE GOAL
Every security control maps to protecting C, I, or A. Understanding which pillar you're defending helps select the right tool.
