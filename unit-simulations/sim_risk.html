// KEY CONCEPTS
RISK FORMULA
Risk = Likelihood × Impact. A low-likelihood event with catastrophic impact can still be high-risk. Both dimensions matter.
RISK PRIORITIZATION
Organizations can't fix everything at once. High-risk items (score 15–25) get immediate attention; low-risk (1–6) may be accepted or monitored.
RESIDUAL RISK
The risk that remains after controls are applied. No system is perfectly secure — the goal is to reduce risk to an acceptable level.
RISK TOLERANCE
Different organizations accept different levels of risk. A hospital has near-zero tolerance for patient data exposure; a school may tolerate more risk.
THREAT vs VULNERABILITY
Threat = who/what could attack. Vulnerability = the weakness they'd exploit. Risk requires both — a threat with no vulnerability is harmless.
RISK TREATMENT
Four options: Mitigate (add controls), Transfer (insurance), Avoid (stop the activity), Accept (acknowledge and monitor).
