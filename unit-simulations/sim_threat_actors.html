// THREAT ACTOR REFERENCE
NATION-STATE
Government-sponsored. High sophistication. Targets: government, military, critical infrastructure. Motive: espionage, disruption, geopolitical advantage.
CYBERCRIMINAL
Financially motivated. Targets: anyone with money or valuable data. Tools: ransomware, phishing, credential theft. Motive: profit.
HACKTIVIST
Ideologically motivated. Targets: organizations they oppose politically. Tools: DDoS, defacement, leaks. Motive: making a statement.
INSIDER THREAT
Current or former employee, contractor, or partner. Has legitimate access. Motive: revenge, financial gain, ideology, or accidental harm.
SCRIPT KIDDIE
Low skill. Uses pre-built tools without understanding them. Targets: anyone vulnerable. Motive: notoriety, curiosity, or mischief.
THREAT MODELING
Analyzing who is likely to attack you, why, and how — so defenses can be prioritized appropriately for your organization.
