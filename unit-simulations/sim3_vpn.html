// KEY CONCEPTS
VPN — WHAT IT DOES
A VPN encrypts all traffic between your device and the VPN server, and hides your real IP from destinations. Your ISP sees only encrypted traffic to the VPN server — not your actual destinations or content.
VPN — WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
A VPN doesn't make you anonymous — the VPN provider sees your traffic. It doesn't protect against malware on your device. It doesn't guarantee privacy if the VPN provider logs your activity or is compromised.
TLS/HTTPS — WHAT IT DOES
TLS encrypts the payload (content, credentials, cookies) between browser and server. An eavesdropper on the same network cannot read the content of HTTPS communications.
TLS/HTTPS — WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
TLS doesn't hide which server you're connecting to (SNI leaks the hostname). It doesn't verify the website is legitimate (phishing sites get TLS certs too). It doesn't protect metadata — timing and volume are still visible.
