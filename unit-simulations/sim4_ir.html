NIST IR LIFECYCLE
Preparation → Detection & Analysis → Containment → Eradication → Recovery → Post-Incident Activity. The AP exam tests this order directly. Containment BEFORE eradication is the most commonly tested sequence rule.
CONTAINMENT BEFORE ERADICATION
You must stop the bleeding before removing the malware. Isolating the infected system prevents further spread while preserving forensic evidence. Removing malware from a still-connected system risks incomplete eradication.
PRESERVE EVIDENCE FIRST
Before any remediation, capture forensic images, memory dumps, and logs. Once you wipe a system or restore from backup, the forensic evidence is gone. Legal proceedings and root cause analysis depend on this data.
LESSONS LEARNED
The Post-Incident phase always ends with a lessons learned review — what happened, what worked, what didn't, what changes prevent recurrence. This closes the loop back to Preparation, making the next response better.
