AP Exam Connection: Unit 5 completes the picture. The FRQ asks students to analyze a device security scenario and recommend controls — but the MCQ tests cryptography (symmetric vs. asymmetric vs. hashing), web application attacks (SQLi, XSS), PKI (certificate chains, CA trust), and data protection (classification, encryption at rest vs. in transit). The SIEM capstone sim integrates all of these into the evidence-based analysis format the exam rewards.
