// KEY CONCEPTS FROM THIS LAB
SPOOFING
Faking a trusted identity — domain name, sender name, or branding — to appear legitimate.
URGENCY TACTIC
Creating artificial time pressure to bypass rational thinking and trigger impulsive action.
SOCIAL ENGINEERING
Manipulating human psychology rather than exploiting technical vulnerabilities.
TYPOSQUATTING
Registering a domain that looks like a trusted one at a glance (ev1te-invitations.com vs evite.com). One character swap is all it takes.
MFA
Multi-Factor Authentication: requires 2+ forms of verification, so stolen passwords alone aren't enough.
DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH
Using multiple layered controls so that failure of one doesn't mean total compromise.
