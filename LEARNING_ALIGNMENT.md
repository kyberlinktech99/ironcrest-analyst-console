# AP Lens, team names, and enrichment

All three activities now share one AP Lens renderer and display their main concept, curriculum objective codes, the same five evidence cards, and a terminology reference. The reference is also available from every case view.

## Curriculum mapping

- Possible Account Compromise: AP 1.2.A–C; Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity sections 2.2, 3.1, 3.4; CYBER.ORG Cybersecurity 1 units 1 and 9.
- Signal Lost: AP 1.3.A–C; Cisco sections 1.4, 2.2, 3.1; CYBER.ORG units 7 and 8.
- Chain Reaction: AP 1.1.A–C with 1.2.A and 1.3.B–C connections; Cisco sections 2.1, 2.2, 4.2; CYBER.ORG units 2, 8 and 9.

These are editorial topic matches, not provider-endorsed course equivalencies. The three activities do not claim to cover every Unit 1 objective. Post-login containment and evidence reasoning extend the introductory concepts into the case investigation.

Verified official sources, September 27, 2026:

- [College Board course and exam description](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-cybersecurity-course-and-exam-description.pdf), printed pages 33–38.
- [Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity](https://www.netacad.com/courses/introduction-to-cybersecurity?courseLang=en-US). Section outline also retrieved through the [official localized course page showing English course content](https://www.netacad.com/zh/courses/introduction-to-cybersecurity).
- [CYBER.ORG Cybersecurity 1](https://cyber.org/cybersecurity-1), current public unit outline.

Course-level links are intentional: learners locate the specified sections after enrollment or through their teacher. Authenticated lesson URLs are not public. Every activity supplies a transfer challenge to deepen learning after recording its final response.
